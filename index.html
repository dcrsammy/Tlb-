How it works

  1. Tell us your idea. Send a request or photos of what you like.
  2. Agree the details. We chat on WhatsApp about fabric, style, price and timing.
  3. Measurements and fitting. In person in Scotland. Guided measuring for the rest of the UK.
  4. We make it. You see progress before it is finished.
  5. Delivery or pickup. Tracked UK delivery, or collect in Scotland.
