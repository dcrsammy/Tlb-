How it works

  1. Tell us your idea. Send a request or photos of what you like.
  2. Agree the details. We chat on WhatsApp about fabric, style, price and timing.
  3. Measurements and fitting. In person in Scotland. Guided measuring for the rest of the UK.
  4. We make it. You see progress before it is finished.
  5. Delivery or pickup. Tracked UK delivery, or collect in Scotland.

Our story

It began with a father who knew how to put an outfit together, and a daughter who was watching. He bought her her first heels. Later, she saw her aunt's sewing machine and started cutting paper into blouses, skirts and trousers.

That paper became cloth. Today OMAQ Apparel makes clothes that are unique to the person wearing them.
