Our story

Where OMAQ Apparel began.

It began with a father who knew how to put an outfit together, and a daughter who was watching. He bought her her first heels.

Later, she saw her aunt's sewing machine and started cutting paper into blouses, skirts and trousers.

That paper became cloth. Today OMAQ Apparel makes clothes that are unique to the person wearing them.
